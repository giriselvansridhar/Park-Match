The product

Two sides, one platform

Finding parking in a crowded city is slow and uncertain, while thousands of driveways sit empty all day. ParkMatch connects the two.

For drivers

Parking sorted before you arrive

  • Map search with price pins, clustering and "search this area". Sort by nearest, cheapest or top rated.
  • Filters for car or bike, budget, covered, CCTV, EV charging, guard and instant book.
  • Digital pass with directions and the host's number, free cancellation, and reviews after each stay.

How a booking works

1 · Search

The driver searches around them, with filters for price, vehicle and amenities.

2 · Request

They pick a time and see the price. The slot is checked against capacity and overlapping bookings.

3 · Confirm

The host accepts from their dashboard, or instant book confirms it automatically.

4 · Park & review

The pass gives directions and the host's contact. After the stay, the driver rates the spot.

Under the hood

How it's built

A server-rendered Django app with no front-end framework. Pages are fast, work on any phone, and all the logic lives in the backend where it can be tested.

Python 3 Django 5.1 SQLite Tailwind CSS Leaflet + OpenStreetMap Vanilla JavaScript Django test suite

Geo search

A bounding-box filter in the database narrows the candidates, then Haversine distance gives the exact radius. Three sort modes and amenity filters run on top.

No double-booking

Every request checks overlapping bookings against the spot's capacity. The check runs again, inside a transaction, when the host accepts.

Secure by default

PINs are hashed with PBKDF2, and a number locks after 5 wrong tries. Role-based guards, ownership checks, CSRF protection and safe redirects cover every action.

Host analytics

Revenue, ratings and occupancy come from ORM aggregations. The 14-day chart is plain HTML and CSS, with a table view.

Generated cover art

A listing without a photo gets an SVG scene (garage, basement, beach, driveway or lot) that matches its description.
